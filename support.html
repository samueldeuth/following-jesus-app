Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in?

Following Jesus uses Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple — there's no separate password to remember. Just tap "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Apple" and use the account you already have.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us at info@followingjesusbook.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from the email address associated with your account, and we'll permanently delete your account and all associated data within a few business days. (An in-app self-service deletion option is coming soon.)

I forgot my password — what do I do?

There's no Following Jesus password to reset. Since sign-in goes through Google or Apple, just recover access to that account directly with Google or Apple if needed.

Something in the app isn't working right

Let us know what happened and what device you're using at info@followingjesusbook.com — screenshots are always helpful.

I have a question about a course I purchased

Reach out to info@followingjesusbook.com with your order confirmation or the email you used at checkout.

I lead a group or church and need help

Church admins and group leaders can email info@followingjesusbook.com for help with your dashboard, adding leaders, or managing students.
